Own: lists of legal and privacy requirements, draft Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, refund and cancellation policy, review and content guidelines, consent flows, data retention and deletion rules.

Do:
- Identify rules that may apply based on where the business and users are (data protection, consumer, tax invoicing, payments, advertising of reviews).
- Specify features needed for compliance: consent checkboxes, data export, account deletion, age checks, content takedown.

Done when: a list of requirements and policy drafts is ready for the owner to send to a lawyer.
