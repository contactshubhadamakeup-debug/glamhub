# GlamHub Marketing Launch Plan

## Overview
- **Launch Type:** Private beta → Public launch
- **Target:** First 30 professionals, 100+ clients in 90 days
- **Budget:** Owner-approved marketing spend

## Phase 1: Pre-Launch (Private Beta - Weeks 1-4)

### Professional Outreach (Goal: 30 professionals)
| Channel | Action | Target |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Direct outreach | Personal LinkedIn/WhatsApp to 50+ professionals in target cities | 15 signups |
| Referral program | Existing beta professionals refer peers (₹500 credit each) | 10 signups |
| Industry groups | Post in makeup artist, hair stylist Facebook/WhatsApp groups | 5 signups |

### Client Acquisition (Goal: 50 clients)
| Channel | Action | Target |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Professional networks | Professionals share portfolio links with their clients | 30 clients |
| Social media | Instagram posts from professionals tagging GlamHub | 20 clients |

### Content
- **Portfolio pages:** SEO-optimized (profession + city)
- **Professional profiles:** Pre-filled with placeholder content
- **Email sequences:** Welcome, onboarding tips, first booking reminder

## Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Contingency |
|------|-------------|
| Low professional signups | Increase outreach, offer extended trial, referral bonus |
| Payment gateway issues | Fallback to test mode, manual invoice processing |
| Negative reviews | Proactive moderation, professional response templates |
| Server overload | Scale Cloud Run max-instances, enable CDN |
| Legal/compliance issue | Pause feature, consult lawyer, owner decision |
